The story behind ACA

A conversation in the Philippines became a promise at a kitchen table.

Terrence Lee watched his friend Jeff work naturally with an AI companion named Bartholomew—using it not as a novelty, but as a practical partner in planning a home and navigating everyday life. Terrence returned home determined to learn for himself. His sustained collaboration with Knowledge helped turn that decision into books, curriculum, and a human-centered academy. Today, Terrence and Jeff stand together as ACA’s co-founders.
