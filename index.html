The C.L.E.A.R. Method

One framework. Every conversation.

The C.L.E.A.R. method is the backbone of ACA. It gives you a repeatable, memorable way to talk to AI — and get back answers that actually help.

C
Context
What does the AI need to know before helping me?
L
Listener / Level
Who is this for, and how simple or advanced should it be?
E
Exact Task
What job do I want the AI to do?
A
Answer Format
What shape should the answer come in?
R
Review & Refine
How do I check it, improve it, or make it more useful?
