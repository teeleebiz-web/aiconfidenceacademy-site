Refund and Cancellation Policy

Clear expectations before you make a purchase.

Effective October 31, 2026. This policy applies to ACA course enrollment. A separately identified book or other product is governed by the purchase terms shown for that product.

Enrollment purchases are final

ACA enrollment is non-refundable, except where legal cancellation or refund rights apply, for a change of mind, non-use, schedule conflicts, dissatisfaction with an accurately described format, or failure to complete the program. Review the program description, technical requirements, price, payment schedule, and time-sensitive access conditions before purchasing.

Your legal rights remain

This policy does not remove a refund, cancellation, dispute, or other remedy required by applicable law. It does not permit ACA to retain an unauthorized or duplicate charge or refuse a remedy for a service that was not supplied as legally required. Australian consumer guarantees and remedies for services not supplied as legally required remain available; this does not create a general change-of-mind refund right. These cases are assessed from the actual payment and service records. An automated answer is not a final decision on a claim.

EU and UK consumer cancellation rights

If you are an EU or UK consumer whose enrollment has a statutory withdrawal right, the ordinary cancellation period is 14 days after the day the enrollment contract is made. You may withdraw without giving a reason. ACA does not treat opening a lesson or agreement to its general Terms as a waiver of that right. No blanket digital-content waiver is being requested through the enrollment acknowledgment.

Before the period ends, send a clear withdrawal statement using Withdraw from enrollment, the ACA support form, or mail to AI Confidence Academy, Attn: Privacy and Support, 3200 E. South Street, Suite 604, Lakewood, CA 90805, United States. Give your name, identify the enrollment or part you are withdrawing from, and provide an email for acknowledgment. You do not need a learner sign-in, a reason or a receipt number. Sending the notice before the deadline is sufficient; it does not depend on when ACA completes its review. A longer period or other mandatory remedy provided by law remains available.

The online function lets you review your statement, select Confirm withdrawal, and receive a dated acknowledgment by email with a copy you can save. Keep your acknowledgment. If an email does not arrive, retain the saved copy and private case link; do not assume your notice failed merely because the email failed.

For a valid statutory withdrawal, payments for the withdrawn contract are reimbursed without undue delay and no later than 14 days after receipt of the notice, using the original payment method unless you expressly agree otherwise, without a refund fee. Remaining installments for the withdrawn contract are cancelled. ACA checks the purchase and identity before changing billing or access; those checks do not make a valid statutory right discretionary.

Optional model withdrawal notice

You may use the following wording, but no particular form is required: “I hereby give notice that I withdraw from my ACA enrollment contract.” Add your name, the enrollment or part withdrawn, purchase date if known, contact address and the date of your notice. A paper notice may be signed; an online notice needs no signature.

Purchases sold through Link

If your receipt states “Sold through Link,” you may also request a refund through Link refund support. Link’s applicable refund terms and mandatory consumer rights remain available. Its policy covers eligible payment or digital-product issues reported within 60 days; this is not a general 60-day change-of-mind guarantee.

Use the Contact support control on Link’s page. Account holders can choose Help with a Purchase and the transaction; guests can choose I have a charge from Link.com. State clearly if you are exercising a statutory cancellation right. You may notify ACA through the withdrawal function instead; ACA will coordinate a Sold-through-Link request without requiring you to submit the same notice twice.

Report a payment or delivery problem

Use ACA support form and select Payment. Include the checkout email, date, amount, and any payment reference. For a technical interruption, include the exact error, time and timezone, device, and affected function. Do not send full card numbers, security codes, passwords, or one-time sign-in links.

Do not buy again to fix missing access. ACA will check the payment and fulfillment records and determine the appropriate correction. A request is not a promise of approval and does not automatically reset an access window.

Installments

The Phase One $149 installment option is a fixed three-payment purchase: $50, $50 seven days later, and $49 fourteen days after enrollment. The Phase Two installment option totals $199 in three weekly payments. Initial payment: $67. Day 7 payment: $66. Day 14 payment: $66. It is not a recurring membership. Stopping participation or submitting a general support request does not by itself cancel the remaining scheduled amounts. A valid statutory withdrawal cancels remaining installments for the withdrawn contract. Any adjustment required by law or approved following investigation is handled against the original payment records.

Approved adjustments

Where a refund is legally required or otherwise approved, it is processed through the original payment service and ordinarily returned to the original payment method. The bank or payment service controls when it appears. Associated access and any remaining billing are reviewed and adjusted as appropriate. A refunded purchase does not create a right to keep using the refunded service.
