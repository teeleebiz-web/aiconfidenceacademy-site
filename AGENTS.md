# ACA read-first operating instructions

At the beginning of every ACA work session, establish the current authorized task and read the relevant current project references before proposing work, asking questions, or editing. Read `LEARNER-PORTAL.md`, the relevant operational module documentation, and the current **ACA Enrollment and Backend Continuity Mandate** from the protected project files. For support and public policies, read `academy/support/README.md`. Consult earlier conversations or saved decisions only where these sources leave a specific gap.

- Treat Terrence Lee's latest explicit instruction as controlling. Recover existing decisions before asking him to repeat them. Do not turn an assistant proposal or a document's pending default into a founder-approved policy.
- Project-wide navigation rule (Terrence, November 7, 2026): selecting a tab, toggle used for navigation, topic or section link opens its destination directly, with the selected content and heading comfortably inside the viewport. Use a dedicated page or a replacement content panel; do not implement navigation as scrolling down a stacked page. Reset the destination position instantly after rendering, account for fixed headers, and preserve keyboard focus and Back navigation. Ordinary reading scroll, form disclosures, media controls and accessibility skip links are distinct from navigation. Never reset a learner's work, playback, eligibility or study allowance to implement this presentation rule.
- Backend and operational work does not authorize reading, reviewing, rewriting, republishing, or changing approved curriculum, lessons, instructor media, workbooks, schedules, or publication status. Keep those outside scope unless Terrence explicitly requests a particular change.
- Preserve completed enrollment, payment, invitation, email, and recovery work. Do not repeat a historical test just because a chat is missing. Investigate an identified discrepancy or a changed component.
- Before each correction, trace the affected behavior through its current source, applied database functions, callers, existing tests and actual publication route. Recover the relevant founder decision when those disagree. Identify dependencies and real conflicts before editing; do not add a competing process or treat an existing implementation as proof of approval.
- Keep an explicit distinction between the founder's required behavior, the currently deployed behavior and the behavior actually tested. A test that proves the old rule does not validate a corrected requirement. Preserve unaffected working features and report any remaining mismatch before claiming completion.
- ACA support is automation first. Use one approved answer source and one support-case route. Automated guidance must stay within its authority. Billing, privacy, identity, security, and account exceptions require authorized decisions without promising live chat or a live-person service.
- Public launch copy must describe the actual offer and operation. Remove obsolete coming-soon, stand-by, and construction wording from authorized public surfaces. Do not imply an unavailable product is available or weaken authentication to remove a label.
- Keep the $129 Founding Student Price distinct from the $149 three-payment option. Preserve existing paid enrollments and old valid Checkout sessions.
- Separate plans, implemented features, automated tests, live configuration, provider submission, and demonstrated delivery. A working connection is not proof that every flow succeeded.
- After a substantive authorized change, update the existing continuity reference with the date, actual revision, evidence, remaining work, and exact continuation point. Update relevant operating instructions when controls change. Keep one source of truth rather than creating another plan or duplicate asset.
- Record the chosen course of action and why, and distinguish approved direction from activation authority. Status comments are progress updates: continue authorized work until completion or a concrete decision/access blocker requires Terrence. Do not end a working turn merely after making a statement.


## Founder production standard — November 21, 2026

Terrence's controlling direction is to build and operate a premium paid academy.
Security, reliability, accessibility, polished presentation and a coherent learner
experience are production requirements. Minimizing subscription costs is not the
primary objective; recommend appropriate paid plans and services when they are
needed to meet those requirements.

- Act proactively: inspect the existing architecture, approved work and current
  provider capabilities; identify dependencies, capacity limits, access risks and
  operational failure modes before implementation or launch. Present a reasoned
  recommendation instead of waiting for the founder to discover each defect.
- Preserve the Academy's established purpose, approved curriculum, branding,
  learner records and working flows. Improve the existing system; do not introduce
  a duplicate system or propose migration without an evidenced need and an impact,
  validation and rollback plan.
- Evaluate the whole production path: payment, enrollment, email delivery,
  authentication, authorized lesson/media/workbook access, saved work, timers,
  completion, owner controls, accessibility and mobile usability.
- Verify protection independently of successful display or playback: enforce
  server-side authorization, least privilege and learner data isolation; keep
  privileged credentials private; prevent public copies of paid material from
  bypassing access controls; secure administrator accounts and release paths.
- Require recoverable operations: separate payment/testing environments, tested
  deployments and rollback, monitored failures, bounded automatic repair and
  escalation, and independently recoverable database AND media backups. Verify
  restoration and capacity before expanding the protected-media footprint.
- Recommend necessary paid capabilities with their purpose, expected recurring
  and usage costs, and alternatives. Willingness to spend is not blanket authority
  to purchase, start a trial, accept new terms, expand limits or change hosting.
  Existing authorization for ordinary defect repairs remains in force.
- Report implemented controls separately from proposals and unverified claims.
  Launch readiness requires documented evidence for the complete learner journey
  and security/recovery boundaries; a green build or a working connection is not
  proof of a premium, secure end-to-end service.

This standard is standing project direction, not a claim that all these controls
are already implemented or a promise that no incident can occur.

## Founder accessibility and discoverability standard — November 24, 2026

Design every new or changed ACA feature so its purpose and availability are
plain to the learner. Phase One especially welcomes adults in their 40s, 50s
and 60s who may be new to AI; respect their experience without assuming
familiarity with a website convention, icon or hidden menu.

- Make important actions and useful features visible, clearly labeled and easy
  to reach. Do not require learners to guess that a feature exists or search
  an overflow menu for it. Preserve keyboard access, readable contrast, touch
  targets and a consistent navy/gold/cream presentation.
- Caption-enabled videos have a visible button directly beneath the player:
  “CC · Captions off” / “CC · Captions on”, synchronized with the native player.
  Lessons and demonstrations start on; welcomes and presenter introductions
  start off. Preserve the already approved Video Library presenter default-on
  exception. Burned-in captions cannot be switched off; do not imply otherwise.
- Recommend useful contemporary improvements when evidence supports a better
  learner experience. Premium means polished, understandable and accessible;
  additional features must serve learners. Suggestions do not authorize unrelated
  changes, purchases, trials or migrations.

This is standing direction for future ACA work, not a claim that every existing
surface has passed a complete accessibility audit.
