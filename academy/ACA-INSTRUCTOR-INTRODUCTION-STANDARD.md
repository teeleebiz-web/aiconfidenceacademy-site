# ACA Instructor Introduction Standard — Founder Approved
**Effective October 9, 2026. Applies to every instructor and future lesson in all Academy phases unless expressly superseded.**

## Opening
Each instructor's spoken introduction must, in warm conversational delivery:
1. Welcome the learner back to the **AI Confidence Academy** (or welcome first-time learners as context requires).
2. Explicitly announce the **phase, journey number, lesson number and exact lesson title** in words, so the learner immediately knows their place.
3. Briefly connect the current learning to the prior lesson or, for a first lesson, orient the learner to the new phase or journey.
4. Describe the immediate learning purpose and what the learner will practice, without duplicating the entire guided lesson.

## Closing the *introduction*
Conclude the instructor's introduction, naturally and consistently, with exactly:

**Let's begin.**

This is the transition into instruction, not the closing line of the entire lesson. Do not end an introduction with “Let's begin the journey,” “Let's learn together,” or an abrupt cut-off.

## Production continuity
Use the approved Academy instructor identity, voice, logo, begin/end/return cards, responsive lesson player, transcripts and accessibility standards. Retain full substantive existing lesson teaching, guided audio, fictional demonstrations and learner-owned workbook/Project Record.

For revisions, generate a new versioned intro asset, verify the actual media and audio, update the corresponding protected lesson's media source and full transcript, and only retire the old media after founder acceptance. Do not use a temporary signed HeyGen URL as a learner link.

**Quality gate:** both the first spoken sentence sequence (correct identity/location/title) and the last spoken words (“Let's begin”) must match the final video. All future lesson production acceptance tests should enforce these requirements.
